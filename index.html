The Math Explained

4/11
1
The ROI Formula
ROI % = (Net Return ÷ Annual Investment) × 100
Agent: ($796K ÷ $121,797) × 100 = ~654%
IBM Bob: ($664K ÷ $150,000) × 100 = ~443%
Blended: ($1,460K ÷ $271,797) × 100 = ~537%
Both solutions recover investment within 9 weeks
2
Payback Period Formula
Payback (weeks) = (Annual Cost ÷ Annual Benefit) × 52
Agent: ($121,797 ÷ $918,000) × 52 = ~6.9 wks ≈ 8 wks (ramp-adjusted)
IBM Bob: ($150,000 ÷ $814,000) × 52 = ~9.6 wks ≈ 9 wks

How Savings Translate to 50% Efficiency

9/11
The ROI% and the efficiency% are different metrics — this is the narrative bridge connecting $1.46M Year 1 savings to hours of manual work released.
1
Establish the Manual Effort Baseline
~98% of Planning Analytics work is currently manual (workshop-confirmed). 750 users × ~40 hrs/week × 48 weeks = ~1,440,000 total annual labour hours in scope.
~1,411,200 hours/year of manual work — the efficiency% denominator
2
Calculate Hours Released by AI in Year 1
PA Agent: 375 active users × 4 hrs/week × 48 weeks = 72,000 gross hours. IBM Bob: ~8,140 developer-hours of realised value.
Total gross hours released Year 1: ~80,140 hours
3
Express as % of Manual Baseline
80,140 ÷ 1,411,200 — using 50% adoption, 15% realisation, and 25–30% conservative scaling.
Year 1: ~5.7% of total manual effort automated
4
The 3-Year Trajectory to 50%
Year 1 ramp → Year 2 adoption deepens → Year 3 full adoption + compounding modernisation flywheel.
$5.2M cumulative net return = the financial expression of this efficiency journey
Why ROI% ≠ Efficiency%
ROI measures return on investment — a 537% ROI means every $1 invested returns $5.37. Efficiency% measures how much total manual workload AI has absorbed. These metrics move in the same direction but are calculated differently.
